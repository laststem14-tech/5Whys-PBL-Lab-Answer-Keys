5 Whys 질문기반 영어 PBL 수업 모범 정답 가이드

지문 A, B, C별 문제 정의, 5 Whys 사다리, 근본 원인 및 해결책 예시

지문 A 모범 정답 (Group 1 & Group 4 | 21번 지문)

주제: 디지털 알림과 집중력
① 한국어 요약:

끊임없는 디지털 알림과 앱 전환으로 인해 현대인들이 깊은 집중을 유지하지 못하고 겉보기 다급함에 빠지는 문제

② 영어 요지 (Main Idea):

This passage says that true effectiveness comes from maintaining deep focus and intentional stillness rather than speed and constant busyness.

Problem Statement (영어 문제 정의):

"Digital notifications and constant app switching make it difficult for people to maintain deep focus."

5 Whys Ladder (사다리 완성 예시)

단계 Why 질문 (영어) 답 (Because~) (영어) F/I (문장)
Why 1 Why is it hard to maintain focus? Because notifications pull our attention in different directions. F [1]
Why 2 Why do notifications pull our attention so often? Because most people switch tasks many times every hour. F [4]
Why 3 Why do people switch tasks so frequently? Because they confuse motion with meaning, assuming that being busy equals being productive. F [6]
Why 4 Why do people assume that being busy equals being productive? Because they treat their work as a continuous "performance of urgency." F [7]
Why 5 Why do people engage in this performance of urgency? Because people lack structured habits and boundaries to value strategic stillness. I (추론)

Root Cause (근본 원인): So, the root cause is that people lack structured habits and boundaries to value strategic stillness.

Solutions (해결책):

  • One way to solve this is to set specific 45-minute notification-free focus blocks during study sessions.
  • We suggest that students should practice intentional stillness by disabling pop-up alerts before starting tasks.

지문 B 모범 정답 (Group 2 & Group 5 | 33번 지문)

주제: 지나친 과제와 전인적 성장
① 한국어 요약:

과도한 숙제로 인해 학생들의 교과 외 활동 및 여가 시간이 제한되어 전인적 성장이 방해받는 문제

② 영어 요지 (Main Idea):

This passage says that school assignments should be coordinated so that students have time for extra-curricular activities to grow into a whole person.

Problem Statement (영어 문제 정의):

"Excessive homework prevents children from engaging in important extra-curricular activities and family leisure time."

5 Whys Ladder (사다리 완성 예시)

단계 Why 질문 (영어) 답 (Because~) (영어) F/I (문장)
Why 1 Why can't children participate in extra-curricular activities? Because excessive homework interferes with their after-school time. F [1]
Why 2 Why is after-school homework excessive? Because school personnel assign homework without coordinating workload together. F [6]
Why 3 Why don't school personnel coordinate assignments? Because individual classes focus mainly on formal academic knowledge. F [3]
Why 4 Why do schools focus heavily on formal academic knowledge? Because they overlook the importance of life skills and leisure time. I (추론)
Why 5 Why do schools overlook leisure time and life skills? Because school districts lacked a systematic policy to regulate and balance overall homework hours. I (추론)

Root Cause (근본 원인): So, the root cause is that school districts lacked a systematic policy to regulate and balance overall homework hours.

Solutions (해결책):

  • One way to solve this is to establish a shared teacher assignment calendar to cap daily homework limits.
  • We suggest that school districts should enforce policy rules requiring coordinated homework schedules.

지문 C 모범 정답 (Group 3 & Group 6 | 36번 지문)

주제: 식습관과 자기 자비
① 한국어 요약:

음식을 엄격하게 제한하는 식습관 방식이 박탈감을 유발하고 오히려 폭식이라는 역효과를 낳는 문제

② 영어 요지 (Main Idea):

This passage says that sustainable healthy eating requires flexibility, moderation, and self-compassion rather than strict food restrictions.

Problem Statement (영어 문제 정의):

"Overly restrictive food rules create intense deprivation and frequently result in uncontrolled overeating."

5 Whys Ladder (사다리 완성 예시)

단계 Why 질문 (영어) 답 (Because~) (영어) F/I (문장)
Why 1 Why does restrictive eating backfire? Because completely forbidding certain foods builds up feelings of intense deprivation. F [4]
Why 2 Why do feelings of deprivation build up? Because people create rigid rules about what they can and cannot eat. F [2]
Why 3 Why do people create such rigid rules? Because they believe that eating well means eliminating all 'bad' foods completely. F [1]
Why 4 Why do people view healthy eating as total elimination? Because they rely on punishment and restriction instead of self-compassion. F [8]
Why 5 Why do people rely on punishment over self-compassion? Because people lack understanding of psychological well-being and moderation in diet. I (추론)

Root Cause (근본 원인): So, the root cause is that people lack understanding of psychological well-being and moderation in diet.

Solutions (해결책):

  • One way to solve this is to allow occasional treats without guilt to maintain psychological balance.
  • We suggest that individuals practice self-compassion and flexible dietary habits rather than rigid food bans.
